Our company

Founded in 2018 to build software that earns its keep

We began as a small team of engineers convinced that most technology projects fail for the same reason: the build starts before anyone has understood the business.

Since then, Development Molecule has grown into a full-service technology and digital services company delivering custom platforms, mobile applications, AI products and growth programmes for organisations across five countries.

Our delivery centres in Noida and Mumbai work in overlap with client teams in North America and Europe, backed by a commercial presence in Chicago. The result is a partner who is technically deep, commercially fluent and available when you need us.

Our Mission

To engineer technology that measurably improves how our clients operate — reducing manual effort, opening new revenue channels and giving leadership teams the data they need to act with confidence. We measure our success by the outcomes our clients report, not by the volume of code we ship.

Our Vision

To be the long-term engineering partner of choice for ambitious mid-market and enterprise businesses worldwide — recognised for research-led thinking, disciplined delivery and the responsible application of artificial intelligence to real business problems.
